Is SOKO Moto real?

Is this a real company or a drop-shipping page?
Real. SOKO Moto is a family-run, Igbo-Nigerian owned import and dealer business operating from our own hub in Guangzhou, China. We buy direct from audited factories, run every unit of every brand through our 40-point inspection bench, and let you watch your own unit verified on video before it ships. No middlemen, no mystery boxes.
Where do the bikes come from?
We buy at source from vetted factories we've audited ourselves, then run every single unit through our 40-point inspection bench in Guangzhou before it's allowed on a ship. No middlemen, no mystery boxes.
Can I see reviews from real riders?
Yes, on the product pages. They're collected by an independent platform after delivery, only from verified buyers. We don't edit them, even the critical ones.

Delivery & returns

How long does delivery take?
Once your unit has cleared the port: Lagos 3-5 working days, free and fully assembled. Nationwide 4-7 working days, with a remote first-charge walkthrough included.
What if I don't like it?
7-day, no-questions return window from delivery. Bike must be in original condition with under 50 km added. Full refund to your original payment method within 5 days.
Do you ship outside Nigeria?
Yes, we ship from our Guangzhou hub worldwide. For Nigerian riders we handle duties, clearing at the port and last-mile delivery on our side, and the guarantee travels with the bike.

Charging & range

How do I charge it?
Plug into any standard 13A wall socket, the same one your fridge uses. Full charge in about 5 hours, or 2 hours with a fast charger.
Is the range real-world or lab-tested?
Real-world, measured in stop-and-go city traffic with a 75 kg rider. 140 km means 140 km of actual city riding, and we'd rather under-promise.
How does the battery swap work?
The SOKO Swap Battery is a hot-swap pack. Lift the drained one out, drop in a charged one, and go in under two minutes. It ships with the SOKO 02 Cargo and is an add-on for most other models. No stations, no queues. Keep the spare charging at home or at work.

Guarantee & service

What does the guarantee cover?
Three layers. A 90-day dealer guarantee on arrival. If your unit doesn't arrive, or isn't what you verified on video, we make it right. Then the factory warranty is passed through to you, per model, usually 12-24 months on battery and motor, printed on every product page. On top of that, lifetime remote support from our team, any time zone.
Who handles a repair if something breaks?
We work with trusted partner workshops across Nigeria and ship parts direct from Guangzhou, so most repairs don't wait on a sea leg. Open a ticket in the member dashboard or on WhatsApp, and we coordinate the whole thing from our side.
