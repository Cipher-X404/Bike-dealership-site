Who you are actually buying from

The man who got tired of buying blind.

Adeola spent a decade importing goods between Guangzhou and Lagos. He watched friends lose money to "verified" listings, bikes that died in a month, and sellers who vanished with the transfer receipt.

In 2021 he made a decision: if he was going to stand between a rider and a machine, he'd stand there properly. SOKO Moto became the dealer that shows you the inspection video, answers at 2am, and owns the bike after it ships.

Today the whole team works out of the Guangzhou hub, with a support desk that speaks your time zone. Sourcing, checking, shipping, and standing behind the six hundred-odd bikes that carry the name.

How we work

From the hub in China
to the street in Lagos.

We work with a short list of trusted factories, the kind that build for markets with real regulations, not just export orders. When a batch arrives, it doesn't move until it has passed our 40-point inspection: frame, brakes, battery, electronics, a ride check on real pavement.

Then it ships, to Lagos, to London, to wherever you are. And if anything isn't right when it lands, our team is on the line before you've opened the crate. That's the whole business model: trust, in writing.

Every bike gets the same 40 points. Same checklist, same camera on every frame, same signature on the sheet. No batch gets a pass.

The journey of a single bike

Four hands.
One sheet. No shortcuts.

Follow the SOKO 01 from the factory floor in Guangzhou to the street in front of you. Every stage is signed. The carousel drives itself, just watch.

01

Sourced at origin

We buy at source from factories we've audited ourselves in Guangzhou. The invoice, the batch code, the contact: all on file before a single unit is booked.

02

Inspected, unit by unit

Forty points, one camera, one sheet, one signature. A bike doesn't ship on a batch average, it ships on its own inspection number.

03

Packed & cleared

Shrink-wrapped, strapped, containered. We clear the customs and track the manifest, so you never have to chase a number across an ocean.

04

Yours, and supported

The sale ends when the wheels start turning. A 90-day arrival guarantee, a factory-backed warranty on every model, and lifetime remote support. That's the other half of the deal.
